Real Property Documents for Everyone

SquareDeal is a free, open-source project that publishes professional-quality forms for real property transactions. Anyone may view, copy, edit, and use the documents. A free Google account is required to make a copy of most templates.

See the documents

Why these forms

Hawaii industry forms grew long, checkbox-heavy, and agent-centric. Copyright also limits who may use some association forms. SquareDeal aims for short, precise contracts that state promises and obligations clearly, with important terms easy to find and a readable timeline.

Key features

  • Free
  • Open source
  • Important info in the right spots
  • Clear transaction timeline
  • Unambiguous language
  • Short and precise
  • Links to relevant statutes
